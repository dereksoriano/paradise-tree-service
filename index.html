Defensible space Paradise

About 100 feet of fuel clearance — plus tighter Town rules

California Public Resources Code section 4291 requires defensible space of 100 feet from each side of a structure in the state responsibility area, not beyond the property line unless another law or local ordinance requires it. Fuel work is typically more intensive closer to the home.

The Town of Paradise adds its own standards in Municipal Code Chapter 8.58 (Defensible Space and Hazardous Fuel Management), including no vegetation within or overhanging 5 feet of a structure and parcel-size fuel-break rules. Those details, with sources, are on the defensible space page. This is general information, not legal advice.

FAQ

Common questions

How much defensible space is required in Paradise?

California Public Resources Code section 4291 requires owners of structures in the state responsibility area to maintain defensible space of 100 feet from each side of the structure, but not beyond the property line unless another law or local ordinance requires it. The Town of Paradise also has its own rules in Municipal Code Chapter 8.58, including a 5-foot noncombustible zone at the structure, fuel-break rules for streets and driveways, and parcel-size rules (entire parcel if 1½ acres or less; at least 50 feet from the property line plus 100 feet from structures if larger). Confirm current details with the Town and CAL FIRE. Sources are listed on the Defensible Space page.
